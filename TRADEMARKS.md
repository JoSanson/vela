# Vela name and logo

The code of Vela is free software under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE): you may use, study, change and
share it, and every copy you distribute stays under the same licence, with its source.

The **name "Vela"** (as a browser name) and the **Vela logo** are not covered by that licence.
They identify the official project, so people know what they are installing and where updates come
from.

## You can, without asking

- Say that your project is "based on Vela", "a fork of Vela" or "compatible with Vela".
- Use the name and logo to talk about the official project: articles, reviews, videos, tutorials,
  conference talks, links to this repository.
- Build Vela from source for yourself, your team or your school, unchanged, and call it Vela.

## You need to rebrand when

- You distribute a modified version (a fork, a build with changes, a bundle with extra software):
  give it another name and another icon, and do not suggest it is the official Vela.
- You sell, rent or put ads around a build of Vela, modified or not.
- You use the name or logo in a product, domain, app store listing or company name.

## Questions

Open an issue or a discussion on this repository; we are happy to say yes to anything that helps
Vela users.
